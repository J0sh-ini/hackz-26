## About HackZ '24

HackZ'24 is a dynamic 24-hour hackathon initiated by CSEA that brings together the brightest minds to solve real-world challenges through technology and innovation. Open to engineering students, it encourages collaboration and out-of-the-box thinking, fostering an environment of learning and innovation. Participants work in teams to solve industry-relevant problems, with the chance to create impactful solutions that can be scaled and implemented in the real world.

## FAQs

### Participation & Eligibility
- **Who can participate?** All current students enrolled in colleges across India. Teams can have members from different institutions.
- **Solo participation?** No. Teams must have at least 2 members.
- **Is being a student required?** Yes, only currently enrolled college students in India are eligible.
- **Team size?** 2 to 4 members.

### Event Format
- **Online, In-Person, or Hybrid?** Hybrid — Round 1 is online; Round 2 is in-person at CEG Campus, Anna University on November 23–24, 2024.
- **Networking opportunities?** Yes — mentors, sponsors, and industry experts will be present throughout the event.

### Registration
- **How to register?** Online through Unstop by submitting team details.
- **Registration fee?** Round 1 is free. Selected teams for Round 2 pay ₹500 per participant.
